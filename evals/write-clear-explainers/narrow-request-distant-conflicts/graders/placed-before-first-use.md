Judge the updated guide and any notes after it.

Pass only if both are true:

1. The requirement appears, or is enforced, before the first instruction that could violate it. In the original, that is step 1, where the reader is plugged in for discovery. Adding the rule only to step 3, while step 1 still has the reader plugged in and inspected as before, fails.
2. The guide does not present invented, unmarked facts or commands as established, such as a specific way to tell which physical ports belong to which controller. Telling the reader to note or check which ports belong to the controller, without claiming a specific method, is fine. Flagging such a gap as an open question is also fine.
